Permission required

RES-Slim needs your approval

Your browser requires a direct click before RES-Slim can request optional access for the feature you enabled.

RES-Slim will return you to the settings console after this request finishes.

Why this is needed

Optional permissions are requested only when a feature needs access your browser has not granted yet, and only for as long as that feature stays on. Declining leaves the rest of RES-Slim working normally. Read more in the browser documentation.
